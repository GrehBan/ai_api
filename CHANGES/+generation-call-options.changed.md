**Breaking:** `IUseCase.__call__` and `IStreamUseCase.stream`, and the abstract methods of `BaseUseCase`, `BaseStreamUseCase` and `BaseCompositeUseCase`, take the options of the call as `**options: Unpack[CallOptions]`. A use case of your own adds that parameter and passes `**options` to `session.send_request` or `session.stream`; without it, type checkers report an incompatible override.
