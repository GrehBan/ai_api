`GenerationResponse.reasoning` and `CompletedEvent.reasoning` hold a summary of the model's reasoning, and `stream()` yields it as `ReasoningDeltaEvent`s, when the request asks for one through `provider_options`: OpenAI's `{"reasoning": {"summary": "auto"}}` (`OpenAIReasoningOptions`, merged with the shared `effort`) or Anthropic's `{"thinking": {"type": "adaptive", "display": "summarized"}}` (`thinking`, a new field of `CreateMessage`). OpenAI's `ReasoningItem` and `ReasoningSummaryTextDeltaEvent` and Anthropic's `ThinkingBlock` and `ThinkingDelta` are modeled.
