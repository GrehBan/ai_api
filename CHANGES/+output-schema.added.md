`GenerationRequest` and `client.generation` take `output_schema=`, a JSON Schema the response `text` then follows as JSON: OpenAI's `text.format` (`json_schema`, `strict: true`, named by `OpenAIGenerationAdapter(schema_name=...)`, `"output"` by default) and Anthropic's `output_config.format`. The schema is sent as it is. `CreateResponse`/`StreamResponse` gain `text` (`TextConfig`) and Anthropic's `OutputConfig` its `format`; `JSONSchema` and `JSONValue` are in `aiollm.types.requests`.
