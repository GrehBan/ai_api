`GenerationRequest` and `client.generation.create()`/`stream()` take `effort=`, an `Effort` (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max`): how much work the model puts into the response, sent as OpenAI's `reasoning.effort` and Anthropic's `output_config.effort`. Left out, nothing is sent and the model's default applies. `CreateResponse`/`StreamResponse` gain `reasoning` (`Reasoning`) and `CreateMessage`/`StreamMessage` `output_config` (`OutputConfig`), as the providers' schemas define them.
