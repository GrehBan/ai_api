Retries are configured with a `RetryPolicy`: the number of attempts (`max_attempts`, still 4 by default), the deadline, the longest `Retry-After` worth waiting for, the backoff (`FullJitterBackoff` by default, or any callable that takes a `RetryState`) and `retry_if`, which can refuse a transient error. Give one to a session or a client as `retry_policy=`, or to a single `send_request` or `stream` call to replace the session's for that call. A policy only narrows what is retried: methods still opt in with `__retryable__`, and only transient errors are retried. If `retry_if` or `backoff` fails, its error keeps the request's error as its `__cause__`.
